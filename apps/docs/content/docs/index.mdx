Clarx helps any codebase become more legible to AI agents — through a versioned standard, a machine-readable manifest, and open-source scoring tools.

## What Clarx is

**The standard** — 27 rules across five pillars: discoverability, boundary clarity, context efficiency, operational guidance, and edit safety. Technology-agnostic. Applies to any language, framework, or monorepo.

**The tools** — `clarx score` analyzes a repo locally or in CI. `clarx init` scaffolds a manifest. `clarx explain` surfaces rationale and fixes for any rule.

**The manifest** — `clarx-manifest.json` tells agents what to skip, where common tasks live, and how to verify changes. The fastest on-ramp to a higher score.
